# HB Plus Workspace - Testing & Verification Checklist

This document provides a systematic verification matrix for QA engineers and developers to test the authentication system, domain validation, session management, and responsiveness.

---

## Verification Matrix

| ID | Scenario | Steps to Execute | Expected Result | Status | Verification Notes |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---:|:---|
| **AUTH-01** | **Authorized Domain Sign-In** | 1. Open `/login`<br>2. Click "Continue with Google"<br>3. Choose an active account on the authorized domain | Sign-in succeeds. User is redirected to `/dashboard`. Displays verified name, authorized email, and "Authenticated" badge. | **VERIFIED** | Verified by temporarily setting `EXPO_PUBLIC_ALLOWED_DOMAIN=gmail.com` with personal Google account (`op861950@gmail.com`) for allow-path verification, then reverted to `hbplus.fit` for production. |
| **AUTH-02** | **Standard Gmail Account Rejection** | 1. Open `/login`<br>2. Click "Continue with Google"<br>3. Choose an account ending in `@gmail.com` | Sign-in is rejected immediately. Zero session created, user signed out immediately. |
| **AUTH-03** | **Other Unauthorized Domain Rejection** | 1. Open `/login`<br>2. Click "Continue with Google"<br>3. Please login using your official domain email."* | **VERIFIED** | Verified via pure unit tests (`npm run test:domain`) covering evil suffixes, prefixes, subdomains, and foreign domains. |
| **AUTH-04** | **Unauthenticated Dashboard Access** | 1. Open a fresh private/incognito window<br>2. | **AUTH-05** | **Session Restoration on Page Refresh** | 1. Log in with a valid account<br>2. | **AUTH-06** | **Session Re-validation for Corrupted / Revoked Sessions** | 1. Authenticate with an invalid email (simulated in storage)<br>2. | **AUTH-08** | **Popup-Blocked Redirect Fallback** | 1. Configure browser to block all popups for localhost / domain<br>2. | **AUTH-09** | **User Logout Execution & Back Protection** | 1. On `/dashboard`, click "Sign Out"<br>2. Click browser Back button | Button enters loading state.
